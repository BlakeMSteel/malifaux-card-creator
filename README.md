# Malifaux Card Creator

Design and print custom cards for Malifaux: Models, Crews, and Upgrades, with a live preview as you build.

## Features

- **Model, Crew, and Upgrade cards** — full front/back preview for Models and Crews, front-only for Upgrades.
- **Card library** — save, load, and delete cards of each type, kept locally so your work persists between visits.
- **Groups** — bundle a Crew with its Models and Upgrades into a named group for easy loading as a set.
- **Import/export** — copy a group to the clipboard as portable text and paste it back in, anywhere, to restore or share it; re-importing the same group updates existing cards instead of duplicating them.
- **PDF export** — export a group as a PDF with one page per card side, ready to print.
- **Inline icons** — type bracketed tokens like `[ram]` or `[melee]` in any text field to render suit, damage, and modifier icons on the card, or insert them with a click from the icon palette.
- **Auto-fit text** — ability and action lists shrink to fit the card instead of overflowing.
- **Mobile friendly** — usable on phone-sized screens.
